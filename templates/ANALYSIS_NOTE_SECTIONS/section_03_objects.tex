\subsection{Missing Transverse Energy}
\label{sec:objects:met}

% AGENT: Fill MET reconstruction details if applicable.
% Include algorithm version and soft-term treatment.

The missing transverse energy ($E_\text{T}^\text{miss}$) is computed
from the negative vector sum of the transverse momenta of all calibrated
physics objects and a soft term accounting for tracks not associated with
any reconstructed object.
